Ideas, evidence & institutions.

I am a second-year IBDP student at ISBL aiming to study Politics, Philosophy and Economics at university.

A little about me

I write about scientific methodology, philosophy, economics and contemporary global politics, while also writing and publishing poetry.

I currently serve as Chief Publications Officer at Turkey's Unheard Voices. I have previously published in EconomiCAL and GündemX, and my poetry has appeared in Dil ve Edebiyat Dergisi and Olağan Şiir Dergisi.

I am also deeply involved in Model United Nations. I served as Secretary-General of the Yeşilköy Anatolian High School MUN Club for a year and currently take on executive roles such as Head of Academy and Head of Crisis at various MUN conferences.

I am particularly interested in the decentralised structure of knowledge, and in exploring to what extent scientific principles can effectively guide the study of human affairs.

Fields of interest

These are not meant as fixed academic labels; they are the questions and traditions I currently return to most often.

Knowledge & Method

Epistemology, philosophy of science, scientific methodology, objectivity, evidence and the limits of methods.

Politics & Conflict

History and contemporary conflict zones, global politics, institutions, legitimacy and political crises.

Economics

Macroeconomics, expectations, monetary policy and the relationship between institutions and economic behaviour.

Literature & Religion

Modern Turkish literature, poetry and religious studies, especially where ideas and human experience intersect.
